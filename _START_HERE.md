## What this is

This repository is the public documentary record of an ongoing judicial review in the High Court of England and Wales, Administrative Court, against the Lord Chancellor (the Secretary of State for Justice) and HM Courts and Tribunals Service.

The claimant — Nadia Zahmoul — is a litigant in person, autistic with diagnosed PTSD. She is unrepresented. The judicial review concerns the administrative handling, by HMCTS and the Ministry of Justice, of court-ordered protective measures and evidential material in proceedings in which she has been a party.

The documents published here are filings, decisions, correspondence, and exhibits drawn from live proceedings. They are made public for the purpose of accountability and verification, in circumstances where multiple submissions to courts, regulators, and parliamentary bodies remain undetermined.

No confidential or third-party material is published.

---

## What is being asked of the courts

The judicial review pleads five grounds. They are reproduced in full in the Amended Grounds of Claim (2 January 2026), which sits in `01_JUDICIAL_REVIEW`. In summary:

1. **Administrative revocation or non-operation of court-ordered protective measures** — disability participation measures under FPR Part 3A and Metropolitan Police Service supervision arrangements
2. **Absence of reasons or administrative audit trail** for the steps taken in (1)
3. **Administrative non-handling of serious material** relating to sworn financial disclosure (the Oxus matter)
4. **Administrative handling of digital evidence in court custody** (the Imerman matter)
5. **Failure to consider cumulative impact** across the above

Three applications are sealed and undetermined as at the date of this guide:

- CPR 3.1(7) reconsideration — sealed 12 March 2026
- Application to rely on further particulars of Ground 1 — sealed 9 January 2026
- Expedition application — sealed 14 April 2026

The claimant is preparing a consolidated application for expedition, an oral hearing under CPR 23.8, restoration of participation measures, and permission to instruct an independent expert.

## Folder map

The archive has three numbered folders forming the spine, plus supporting folders organised by document type.

**Spine — read in order:**

- **`00_PUBLIC_RECORD`** — serialised public-record statements distributed to government, parliament, opposition, and media. Days 1, 2, and 3 published to date; further days to follow.
- **`01_JUDICIAL_REVIEW`** — procedural file of the live judicial review (AC-2025-LON-004293). Filings, sealed orders, court correspondence, in chronological order from October 2025.
- **`02_UNANSWERED_SUBMISSIONS_INSTITUTIONAL_SILENCE`** — submissions to courts, regulators, and parliamentary bodies that have received no substantive response. Each sub-folder contains the original submission, supporting documents, and a notification record.

**Supporting folders (document type):**

- `CORRESPONDENCE` — institutional correspondence outside the JR procedural file
- `COURT_DECISIONS` — orders and judgments from the Family Division, Court of Appeal, and other courts
- `COURT_FILINGS` — earlier filings, pre-dating the numbered spine
- `CRIMINAL_REFERRALS` — referrals to the Metropolitan Police and other criminal-justice bodies
- `MEDICAL` — clinical letters and medical records relevant to participation measures
- `PARLIAMENTARY` — submissions to parliamentary committees and ministers
- `PRESS` — press correspondence record
- `TIMELINE` — chronological reference materials
