# 02 — UNANSWERED SUBMISSIONS / INSTITUTIONAL SILENCE

This folder holds the public record of formal correspondence and analytical material I have placed before public institutions in the period October 2025 to May 2026, where the institution to which it was sent did not produce a substantive response.

The purpose is preservation. Each submission represents formal analytical work placed before a named institution exercising a public function. Where the institution did not engage, the analytical work risks being lost into the institutional silence that received it. This folder removes that risk by publishing the submissions themselves — not summaries, not paraphrases — alongside the attachments as they were sent.

---

## How this folder is organised

Each unanswered submission lives in its own subfolder, named with the date and the recipient:

```
20260501_LordsCommittee_EX107/
20260415_JusticeCommittee_ConsolidatedPosition/
20260413_ParliamentFollowUp_GroundsEngagement/
20260409_ACO_N244Expedition/
20260407_JusticeCommittee_FormalSubmission/
...
```

Inside each subfolder:

- **`00_email_body.pdf`** — the email itself, formatted as a clean print-out: From, To, CC, Date, Subject, full body, attachment list, response status. The `00_` prefix sorts it to the top of the folder.
- **All attachments** — every file that was sent with the email, with the original filename preserved.

A reader who opens any subfolder sees the email body first, then every attachment. Everything that was placed before the institution that day is one click away.

---

## The master document

A separate document, **`UNANSWERED_SUBMISSIONS_master.pdf`**, sits at the root of this folder. It is the index. Each entry summarises one subfolder: date, named recipient(s), subject, brief substance, attachment list, response status, days elapsed.

The master document is built by harvesting metadata from the subfolders. It is updated as further unanswered submissions are added.

## Reader notes

The submissions in this folder are reproduced as they were sent. The institutions named were the institutions addressed. The recipients listed were the recipients listed. The dates are the dates of transmission. None of the content is hypothetical, reconstructed, or retrospective. It is the documentary record of work that exists, was sent, and was not engaged with.

## Use

The submissions are placed on the public archive for verification, reference, and such use as institutions, journalists, parliamentary scrutiny, legal practitioners, and the public consider appropriate. No confidential or private third-party material is published.
